Medic character

SciFi Medic – Rigged by Tony Flanagan, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Modified for VESPERWOUND: uniform scale and ground normalization, Blender glTF re-export, geometry compression, texture compression and mipmaps. Character appearance, equipment and rig are supplied by the original author. The downloaded files contain no animation clips.

The original asset remains available under CC BY 4.0. Our game terms do not restrict the rights granted by that license. No endorsement by the author is implied.

Player animations

Walk, jog, idle, jab, cross punch, Ward gesture, roll, hit and death from Universal Animation Library by Quaternius, with animation contributions by Gonzalo Furnier. Licensed under CC0 1.0. Retargeted to Medic’s supplied rig; horizontal travel removed and keyframes compressed. These are additional Phase 2 and Phase 3 animations, separate from the supplied Medic asset.

Ash Quay materials

Materials from Poly Haven and ambientCG are released under CC0. Full author, source and modification records are included with the project's art provenance.

Enemy character and supplied animations

Zombie Number 7 – Animated by Tony Flanagan, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Modified through uniform scale/ground normalization, in-place hip motion, regional geometry reduction, Meshopt and KTX2 compression. Attack, Idle, Walk, Scream and Death originate from supplied clips; Attack was subsequently retimed with held anticipation and planted feet for VESPERWOUND; no additional animation source was added for this enemy. Original appearance and rig belong to the credited author. No endorsement is implied, and game terms do not restrict the rights granted by CC BY 4.0.

Concrete Floor Worn 02 by Dimitrios Savva, under CC0 1.0. Adapted with metre-scaled UVs, tint and texture compression for the district paving.

Ash Quay architecture

Modular Factory Facade by James Ray Cock and Modular Industrial Pipes 01 by Jorge Camacho, both under CC0 1.0. Selected modules composed into the intake; transforms adapted, textures compressed and geometry batched. Original pressure machinery, signs, skyline and dressing authored for VESPERWOUND. Full acquisition and modification record.

Steel baton and armed actions

Original VESPERWOUND steel baton model. Armed actions adapt the acquired Quaternius Universal Animation Library under CC0 1.0, with authored swings, hand grips and offline baked arm/thumb corrections. These additions are separate from Tony Flanagan’s supplied Medic asset.
