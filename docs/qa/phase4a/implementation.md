# Phase 4A implementation

Training is the default. Start encounter loads only the selected enemy tier, replaces training posts with one zombie and disables the vent. Restart encounter resets both combatants, pressure, positions, queued actions and generations. Return to training releases the enemy model and restores targets and vent. No later archetypes or hordes are enabled.

`EncounterSimulation` owns a `PlayerSimulation`, enemy states, fixed-step contact ordering, navigation fields and one attack slot. The session owns its event queue. Entity handles include a generation; attack instance IDs accompany hit, damage, block and warning events. Presentation consumes states and events and cannot change damage. Quality reloads replace visuals transactionally around the same simulation.

The enemy uses 120 health, 100 posture, 1.5 m/s movement and a 20-damage strike. Windup/active/recovery last 650/120/900 ms. Facing stops tracking for the final 250 ms of windup. Ordinary hits produce brief presentation reactions without cancelling a committed attack. Stagger cancels it, exposes for two seconds and releases the slot. An eligible heavy consumes exposure for double damage. Contacts are collected before resolving Medic's damage, preserving simultaneous trades even when Medic's hit defeats the attacker that tick.

Navigation is a clearance-tested 0.25 m grid on the ground floor, with four-neighbour routes and a shared target-directed distance field planned at most 10 Hz. Direct segments require expanded obstacle clearance. Disc sweeps constrain movement and knockback against solids, other combatants and the player. Unreachable targets cause return after two seconds; the eight-metre spawn leash returns without healing or teleporting. Three instances exist only behind the development diagnostic command and in tests.

Medic has no automatic attack step. Sampled jab/cross wrist extension is about 0.645/0.65 m. The CPU capsule reaches 0.55/0.58 m with a 0.12 m fist radius and 0.30 m target hurt radius: aligned far boundaries are 0.97/1.00 m. Side and vertical misses are rejected, and collision obstruction is tested. Zombie's strike uses a 0.80 m reach and 0.08 m radius against a 0.28 m player hurt radius, with windup beginning below 1.05 m. Supplied Attack samples at 42–45% put the striking wrist about 0.73–0.81 m forward. Its clip is mapped onto fixed combat windows; runtime bone positions never resolve damage.

The saved Walk clip is in place. Sampled low-foot horizontal speeds are approximately 0.422/0.376 m/s; the authored 0.405 m/s cycle reference scales playback to simulation speed. This reduces sliding but is not a contact solver: the supplied shuffling gait, turn transitions and knockback retain some foot drift. Review actual captures and the demo before accepting the art/contact gate.

Original ZIP and GLB remain untouched under `art/imports/zombie7`; hashes and licensing are in `art/enemy-provenance.json`. The package supplied GLB and textures, not a Blender file. `art/source/zombie7-master.blend` is the packed editable import. It preserves native source geometry, supplied skeleton/materials and all twelve named actions; horizontal hip translation is removed, with vertical motion retained. Tier modifiers weld duplicate seam vertices before anatomically weighted reduction. Mobile has visible faceting in close-ups; approval is based on source preservation and gameplay readability, not a technical pass alone.

`node tools/build-enemy.mjs` exports the saved master without saving it, verifies its hash is unchanged and applies Meshopt plus semantic KTX2 compression. Normal `npm run assets:build` also exports the enemy. One-time import/repair tools are explicit commands and do not run during normal builds. Desktop/mobile ceilings are 6K/2K triangles and 2K/1K textures; native textures are never upscaled.

Optional load cancellation aborts fetches and disposes late results. Failure retains training with retry. Settings, inspection, background suspension and portrait pause clear held/queued input and freeze CPU combat time. Enemy resources are released on return/session disposal; cloned skeletons are owned separately from shared geometry/material handles. Impact particles and synthesized sounds reuse bounded pools. Reduced Motion removes camera impulses and strong reactions/flashes.
